Where focus lives

A focused text box in the builder currently gets two signals at once: its underline turns black, and a red rectangle is drawn around the whole control. So a field that is a line when you are not in it becomes a box when you are. This page shows three ways of settling that. Pick by tabbing, not by reading.

Press Tab to move between the controls below. The focus signal only appears for the keyboard — that is deliberate and stays true in all three. Then click one with the mouse. Today the underline also darkens on a click while the rectangle does not, so the two disagree about what focus even is. Use ← → or the bar at the bottom to switch variant.

What each one costs

A — the line owns it. Fits a design where a field is a line you write on, and nothing ever changes shape. The ring can never strike the hint or overhang the column, because on text controls there is no ring. Cost: less conventional, and the signal is thinner.

B — the box owns it, outside. The most familiar treatment, and the easiest to see. Cost: every field changes shape when you reach it, and on a full-width control the ring is pushed outside the column.

C — the box owns it, inside. Keeps the familiar box but draws it within the control's own bounds, so it can never strike the hint or overhang. Cost: on a filled button the ring has to sit on the fill, which is a different look from the outlined controls.

In all three, selection and focus stop sharing one geometry — a chosen swatch holds its mark inside while focus sits outside it, so the two can be read at once.
